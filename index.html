Notebook tools

Small helpers for a paper notebook. Each one works in the browser, on your phone and offline.

  • Start Putting something off? Smallest step, five seconds, six minutes.
  • Goals Set a goal in your notebook, then check in every month.
  • Defuse A task grew too big in your head? Shrink it, then the first move.
  • Reset Something went wrong? A short way back to what you control.
  • Reviews Checklists for the morning, evening, week, month and chapter.
